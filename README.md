A game where your grandmother has passed away but can still talk to you through her tomb (because why not), and she's kind and tries to help you but her instructions are not always very clear and often do not mention important parts (the theme, you'll understand below)
There are three levels:

### Level 1
You have to collect 10 coins scattered around in a big, mostly vertical area with jumps, obstacles and things to overcome before reaching every coin (*they will be useful later*)

Granny tells you to go collect them but doesn't mention the hurdles (*nor does she mention the fact that there are ten thousand coins right behind you that you can simply take without doing all the parkour stuff if you find out how to fart (with the key or button being different every time you restart the game)*)

### Level 2
Granny now appears as a ghost and follows you around, telling you to go buy 8 cans of soda to make a staircaise of 6 cans to reach the top of a vending machine at night to fetch the jar with her ashes that was hidden on top, so that she can revive

But what she doesn't tell you is that there's a demon protecting that jar and you will have to beat it in order to retrieve her ashes (*she was too scared that you could fall on the ground and scratch your knees, as a ghost she can't kiss it to make the pain go away (*you are 41 years old*)*)

You can throw various projectiles at the demon during the bossfight to inflict damage to it, and you have to avoid its attacks as they inflict random damage and can sometime instantly kill you, but the bets way to win is to drink the two remaining soda to make a Super Burp that will disintegrate it into a sweet potato that you have to throw on the jar to purify it and resurrect Granny

### Level 3
Your grandma's now alive again !\
She'll bake you cookies as usual, and advise you to eat them slowly, but you'll eat them as quickly as possible anyway and fucking die of excitement as these cookies were too damn good

This will make granny sad, so you'll have to run away from her as she transformed into Ball Granny and is rolling towards you, breaking trees and throwing cows and pigs away if they're in her way, with the only escape being to possess her and make her eat a cookie to go back in time in the level 1 but with both of you alive, hug her, then dance with Shrek and Donkey with [Shreksophone](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W2dxblz6m54) playing, before a nuclear explosion happens somewhere in the distance
